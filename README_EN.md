# Society of Mutual Creation

**An open international project for discussing, developing and implementing the principles of a decentralized society based on mutual creation.**

[Русская версия](README_RU.md) · [English](README_EN.md)

## About the project

**Initiator and author: Artyom Goldaev.**

The Society of Mutual Creation is a proposed voluntary, decentralized association of people and autonomous collectives. Its foundational ideas include meeting real needs, jointly creating goods, developing human capabilities, preserving the natural and material foundations of life, and preventing the concentration of power and domination.

This repository is both an open research and development workspace and a place to build an international community around the concept. It is intended for discussing the idea, reviewing its foundational principles, proposing amendments, and collaboratively developing the mechanisms and procedures required for practical implementation.

The project is a proposal for discussion and development. Publication here does not mean that the principles have been finally agreed upon or adopted by any community.

## What we want to develop together

The current Postulates establish the value-based and logical foundations of the proposed society. They do not yet prescribe detailed operating procedures. Further work may include:

- coordination between autonomous people and collectives;
- decision-making and delegation without creating a permanent center of power;
- prevention of the concentration of influence and control over critical resources;
- resource and energy accounting;
- needs assessment and production planning;
- distribution of basic and additional goods, including under scarcity;
- verification of information and protection against manipulation;
- dispute resolution, accountability, and safeguards against abuse;
- transparent, understandable and contestable use of digital systems and artificial intelligence.

Any proposed mechanism should be assessed for consistency with the Postulates as a whole.

## How to participate

- Use **GitHub Discussions** for questions, general ideas, interpretation, and open-ended debate.
- Use **Issues** for specific problems, proposed amendments, translation discrepancies, or tasks that need to be tracked.
- Use **Pull Requests** when you have a concrete text change to propose.

Please explain the problem a proposal addresses, how it would work, who or what it affects, and what risks or unintended consequences it may create. Disagreement and criticism are welcome when expressed respectfully and supported by reasoning.
