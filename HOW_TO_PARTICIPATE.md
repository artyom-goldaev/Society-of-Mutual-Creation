Welcome to the open space for the collaborative development of the Society of Mutual Creation!

Anyone can contribute an idea, ask a question, share knowledge, discuss existing proposals, point out problems, or join practical work.

**You do not need to be a programmer or have previous experience with GitHub.** Your knowledge, experience, ideas, and willingness to participate are what matter.

---

## 1. Getting Started

First, open [Society of Mutual Creation — Open Development](https://github.com/users/artyom-goldaev/projects).

You will see the shared workspace for developing the project.

We recommend starting with three steps:

1. Read the [Discussion Guide / Навигация по обсуждениям](https://github.com/artyom-goldaev/Society-of-Mutual-Creation/discussions/1).
2. Take a look at the questions and initiatives already being worked on.
3. Choose a topic that interests you or propose your own.

---

## 2. Navigating the Project

The project has two main views. You can switch between them using the tabs at the top of the workspace.

**Open Board / Общая доска**

This is the main view of the project. All cards are arranged by stages of work.

Here you can see:

- which ideas have just been submitted;
- which questions are being discussed;
- which proposals are being refined;
- what is awaiting review;
- what participants are already working on;
- which tasks have been completed or put on hold.

**All Contributions / Все инициативы**

This is a complete list of all cards in a table format.

It is convenient for browsing and finding materials by direction, type of work, priority, and status.

### What the Card Fields Mean

- **Status / Статус** — the current stage of an initiative.
- **Direction / Направление** — the area of the project to which it belongs.
- **Type / Тип работы** — idea, question, research, proposed solution, document, and other options.
- **Priority / Приоритет** — how urgently or importantly an issue needs attention.
- **Assignees / Исполнители** — participants who have voluntarily taken responsibility for specific work.
- **Labels / Метки** — additional tags used to search for and group cards.

---

## 3. What the Statuses Mean

Cards move between stages as the work develops.

- **Ideas & Proposals / Идеи и предложения** — a new idea, initiative, or question.
- **Discussion / Обсуждение** — opinions, arguments, and alternative proposals are being gathered.
- **In refinement / Проработка** — participants are examining the issue in detail and developing possible solutions.
- **Awaiting review / На согласовании** — a prepared result has been submitted for review.
- **In progress / В реализации** — agreed work is being carried out in practice.
- **Done / Завершено** — the work associated with the card has been completed.
- **On hold / Отложено** — work has been temporarily paused.

Important: the status “Done” means that a specific piece of work has been completed. It does not, by itself, mean that a new Postulate has been adopted or a proposal approved. A separate review and approval process is in place for that purpose.

## 4. How to Propose an Idea or Create a New Topic

If you want to propose a new idea, raise a question, or draw attention to a problem:

1. Open the [Society of Mutual Creation](https://github.com/artyom-goldaev/Society-of-Mutual-Creation) repository.
2. Go to the **Issues** section.
3. Click **New issue**.
4. Enter the topic title in both English and Russian.
5. Describe your idea, question, or problem.
6. Click **Submit new issue**.

Where possible, include both language versions: English first, followed by Russian.

You do not need to propose a complete solution straight away. You can simply raise a question that you believe deserves attention.

Once created, the card can be added to the overall Project.

---

## 5. How to Participate in Existing Discussions

If you are interested in a card that has already been created:

1. Open it by clicking its title.
2. Read the description. At the beginning of the card, you will find links to the English and Russian versions.
3. Scroll down to the comment field.
4. Write your opinion, arguments, questions, or proposals.
5. Click **Comment**.

You can reply to other participants, add to their arguments, and suggest alternative solutions.

Disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome.

---

## 6. The Difference Between Issues and Discussions

The project uses two tools for different purposes.

**Discussions / Обсуждения** are a space for open conversation and the exchange of opinions and arguments on important questions.

**Issues / Карточки инициатив** are where specific questions, proposals, problems, and tasks that require attention or collaborative work are recorded.

For example, Discussions can be used to explore in detail whether a society can function without money, while the corresponding Issue can collect the key questions, research, and proposals related to that topic.

You do not need to create a new Discussion if the topic already exists.

---

## 7. How to Join Practical Work

If you want to do more than discuss ideas and take a direct part in developing the project:

- choose a card that interests you;
- read what needs to be done;
- leave a comment and offer your help;
- if you wish, take responsibility for a specific task.

If a card has an **Assignees / Исполнители** field, a participant can be assigned to it after voluntarily agreeing to take on the work.

This does not grant management authority or the right to make decisions unilaterally.

---

## 8. A Few Important Principles

We want the project to remain an open space for mutual creation.

Therefore:

- Everyone can propose ideas and ask questions.
- Any proposal can be discussed and criticised.
- You do not have to agree with the author of the concept or with other participants.
- Arguments, facts, and well-grounded alternatives matter.
- Participation is voluntary. No one is required to take on ongoing commitments.
- Labels, priorities, and assignees are used to organise work, not to establish a hierarchy.
- Changes to the concept and the Postulates require a separate review and approval process. Moving a card or changing its status does not replace that process.

Please respect other participants and try to explain your point of view.
