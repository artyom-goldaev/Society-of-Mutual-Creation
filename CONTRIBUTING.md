# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in the Society of Mutual Creation.

This repository is an open workspace for improving the concept and developing practical mechanisms consistent with its Postulates.

## Ways to contribute

- **Discuss:** raise questions and explore ideas in GitHub Discussions.
- **Report an issue:** identify unclear wording, contradictions, risks, missing procedures, or translation differences.
- **Propose a change:** submit a Pull Request with a focused edit and an explanation of its purpose.
- **Develop mechanisms:** propose procedures, models, diagrams, examples, or prototypes in separate files or discussions.

## Principles for proposals

Please include, where relevant:

1. The Postulate(s) or section concerned.
2. The problem or practical situation being addressed.
3. The proposed rule or mechanism.
4. How it would operate in practice, including roles and decision boundaries.
5. How it avoids coercion, domination, and concentration of power.
6. Possible failure modes, abuse scenarios, and safeguards.
7. Resource, environmental, and human consequences.
8. Any corresponding change needed in the other language version.

## Changes to foundational documents

Please avoid silently changing the meaning of an adopted or under-review principle. Explain the rationale for every substantive edit. For bilingual changes, keep the Russian and English texts aligned and flag any uncertainty rather than guessing.

## Discussion conduct

Be respectful. Criticize ideas, arguments, and mechanisms rather than people. Distinguish facts, assumptions, interpretations, and personal opinions. Explain disagreements and remain open to revising your own proposal.

## Scope

The Postulates are intended to remain an interconnected system. A proposal should be considered in relation to the whole document, not only the clause it changes. Practical efficiency or majority support alone does not justify weakening human dignity, freedom, access to basic goods, or safeguards against domination.

## No automatic endorsement

A comment, issue, or pull request is a proposal for consideration. It does not become part of the project principles merely by being submitted or receiving reactions. The project owner/community should make the status and rationale of decisions transparent.
